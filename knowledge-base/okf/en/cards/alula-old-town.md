# Overview

Located at the narrowest part of the AlUla valley, **AlUla Old Town** is a restored mudbrick settlement inhabited continuously from the 12th century until the late 20th century. The village comprises over **800 tightly packed mudbrick dwellings**, 500 shops, and shaded alleyways.

---

# Highlights & Attractions

* **AlUla Citadel (Fort):** A hilltop fortress originating in the 6th century BCE (renovated during Islamic periods) offering panoramic vistas over the old mudbrick roofs and palm groves.
* **Incense Road & Old Town Market:** A pedestrianized strip featuring local artisan boutiques, specialty coffee roasters, and traditional crafts.
* **Heritage Dining:** Features fine dining and traditional Saudi eateries seamlessly integrated into mudbrick architecture, including Suhail and farm-to-table pop-ups.
* **Guided Rawi Tours:** Local storytellers ("Rawi") guide visitors through restricted heritage zones, describing neighborly roof-connected living and ancient oasis water management.

---

# Visiting Rules

* **Access:** The main market street and restaurants are accessible for free strolls; guided Rawi tours are required to enter the preserved mudbrick labyrinth and fortress interior.
