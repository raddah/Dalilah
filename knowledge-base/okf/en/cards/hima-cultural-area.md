# Summary

Hima Cultural Area lies in south-west Saudi Arabia on an ancient caravan route. It contains rock art and inscriptions in several scripts, together with historic wells.

# Verified claims

- `claim.hima.location`: The property is in an arid mountainous area of south-west Saudi Arabia.
- `claim.hima.rock-art`: Its rock record documents cultural continuity spanning about 7,000 years.
