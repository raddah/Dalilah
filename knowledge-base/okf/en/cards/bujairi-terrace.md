# Summary

**Bujairi Terrace** is a 15,000-square-meter premium culinary destination in ad-Dir'iyah, situated on the eastern bank of Wadi Hanifah facing the UNESCO World Heritage citadel of At-Turaif.

---

# Highlights & Offerings

* **Panoramic At-Turaif Overlook:** Offers outdoor terrace dining directly facing the illuminated mudbrick palaces of At-Turaif, particularly spectacular during golden hour and nighttime lighting.
* **World-Class Culinary Concepts:** Hosts over 20 international Michelin-starred brands alongside high-end Saudi concepts (e.g., Maiz, Takya, Angelina, Hakkasan, Villa Mamas).
* **Wadi Hanifah Integration:** Surrounded by palm groves, walking trails, and traditional Najdi water features.

---

# Access Guidance

* **Diriyah Pass & Reservations:** Access requires a pre-booked Diriyah Pass or a confirmed restaurant reservation via Diriyah.sa or official reservation apps.
