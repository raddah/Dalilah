# Summary

Uruq Bani Ma'arid is a natural World Heritage property at the western edge of the Empty Quarter, conserving a large desert landscape and varied habitats.

# Verified claims

- `claim.uruq.location`: The property lies at the western edge of the Empty Quarter.
- `claim.uruq.wildlife`: The record notes reintroduction of Arabian oryx and Arabian sand gazelle.
