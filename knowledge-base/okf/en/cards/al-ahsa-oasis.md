# Summary

Al-Ahsa Oasis is a World Heritage cultural landscape in the eastern Arabian Peninsula. It includes gardens, canals, springs, wells, a drainage lake, historic buildings, urban fabric, and archaeological sites.

# Verified claims

- `claim.al-ahsa.composition`: The property combines natural, urban, and archaeological components.
- `claim.al-ahsa.palms`: UNESCO records about 2.5 million date palms.
