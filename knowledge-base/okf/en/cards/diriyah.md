# Overview

**ad-Dir'iyah** is the historic capital and birthplace of the First Saudi State, founded in 1727 CE. Located along the lush banks of **Wadi Hanifah** northwest of Riyadh, Diriyah stands as a supreme symbol of Saudi identity, governance, and traditional Najdi mudbrick architecture.

---

# Key Landmarks

* **At-Turaif District:** UNESCO World Heritage site (inscribed in 2010), serving as the citadel and seat of government during the First Saudi State. Features palatial mudbrick complexes and historic mosques.
* **Salwa Palace:** The largest palace complex in At-Turaif, spanning over 10,000 square meters. It served as the primary residence and royal seat of the Imams of the First Saudi State.
* **Bujairi Terrace:** A world-class dining enclave overlooking the illuminated At-Turaif ruins across Wadi Hanifah, featuring Michelin-starred international concepts alongside authentic Saudi hospitality.
* **Al-Bujairi District:** Historically the center of Islamic learning and scholars, now restored into a vibrant cultural parkland.
* **Wadi Hanifah:** The iconic green valley cutting through Diriyah, featuring landscaped palm groves and walking trails.

---

# Architectural Style

Diriyah exemplifies **Najdi Mudbrick Architecture**:
* Constructed using sun-dried mud bricks, straw reinforcement, and palm-log roof beams.
* Decorative triangular crenellations and geometric wall relief patterns.
* Intricately carved wooden doors painted in traditional Najdi motifs.

# Access & Logistics

* **Location:** Located approximately 15–20 minutes northwest of central Riyadh via King Khalid Road.
* **Ticketing:** Access to Bujairi Terrace and At-Turaif requires a Diriyah Entry Pass booked online via the official Diriyah website or app.
