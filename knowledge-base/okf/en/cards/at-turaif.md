# Summary

**At-Turaif** is the citadel and historic core of ad-Dir'iyah, inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage list in 2010. Perched on a high plateau above Wadi Hanifah, At-Turaif represents one of the world's most significant monumental mudbrick architectural complexes.

---

# Palaces & Architectural Highlights

* **Salwa Palace:** The principal palace complex of At-Turaif, comprising seven multi-story mudbrick units built across different historical phases. It served as the central residence and administrative seat of the Imams of the First Saudi State.
* **Saad bin Saud Palace:** A grand Najdi mansion featuring a central courtyard surrounded by massive mudbrick pillars, housing displays of traditional tools and artifacts.
* **Nasir bin Saud Palace:** Restored to host archaeological exhibits detailing excavation finds and architectural restoration techniques.
* **Bait Al-Mal (Treasury) & Grand Mosque:** The administrative center for revenue collection, situated next to the historic mosque of Imam Mohammad bin Saud.
* **Diriyah Museum:** An interactive museum inside Salwa Palace showcasing the chronology of the First Saudi State and traditional Najdi master-craftsmanship.

# Visitor Experience

* **Cultural Guides:** Saudi heritage guides offer immersive historical tours describing royal governance and architectural restoration.
* **Night Projection Shows:** Sound and light projection mapping shows illuminate the facades of Salwa Palace in the evening.
