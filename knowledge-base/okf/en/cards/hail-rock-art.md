# Summary

This serial World Heritage property comprises Jabal Umm Sinman at Jubbah and Jabal al-Manjor and Raat at Shuwaymis, with extensive rock art and inscriptions.

# Verified claims

- `claim.hail.components`: The property has principal components at Jubbah and Shuwaymis.
- `claim.hail.timespan`: Human and animal representations at Shuwaymis cover about 10,000 years of history.
