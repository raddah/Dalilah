# Overview

**Historic Jeddah** (known locally as **Al-Balad**) is a world-renowned heritage district on the eastern shore of the Red Sea, inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage site in 2014. Established in the 7th century CE as a major Indian Ocean trade port, Al-Balad served historically as the primary maritime **gateway for pilgrims arriving by sea en route to the Holy City of Makkah**.

---

# Architectural & Heritage Landmarks

* **Roshan & Coral Architecture:** Multi-story tower houses constructed from Red Sea coral limestone, adorned with intricate, hand-carved **teakwood Roshan windows and overhanging balconies** engineered to maximize sea breeze ventilation and private shade.
* **Nasseef House (Beit Nasseef):** A landmark 7-story merchant mansion built in 1872 CE. It served as the royal residence of **King Abdulaziz ibn Saud** upon entering Jeddah in 1925. Features wide interior ramps designed for pack animals and Jeddah's oldest planted neem tree.
* **Historic Merchant Palaces:** Meticulously restored palatial residences including **Beit Sharbatly, Beit Baeshen, Beit Zainal, and Beit Matbouli**.
* **Historic Mosques:** Houses 7th-century Islamic heritage sites, including **Al-Shafi'i Mosque** (Jeddah's oldest mosque featuring a rare minaret), **Al-Ma'amar Mosque**, and **Othman bin Affan Mosque**.
* **Souk Al-Alawi & Souk Al-Nada:** The oldest traditional open-air bazaars in Jeddah, stretching through stone alleyways filled with spices, incense, textiles, dates, and traditional crafts.

---

# Culture & Living Heritage

* **Walking & Photography Tours:** Pedestrian strolls through historic quarters (*Harat Al-Yaman, Harat Al-Mazloom, Harat Al-Sham, Harat Al-Bahr*).
* **Hejazi Culinary Heritage:** Traditional food stalls and heritage cafes offering local delicacies such as Manto, Yaghmoush, liver sandwiches, and Subia.
* **Season of Ramadan in Al-Balad:** Annual cultural festivals illuminating the coral facades with projections and traditional performing arts.

---

# Visiting Logistics

* **Location:** Located in central Jeddah, minutes from the Red Sea Corniche and Jeddah Islamic Port.
* **Pedestrian Access:** Al-Balad is primarily a pedestrian zone serviced by electric golf carts for elderly visitors and guided heritage tours.
