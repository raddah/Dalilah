# Summary

Al-Balad hosts hundreds of historic merchant residences constructed from Red Sea coral blocks. **Nasseef House (Beit Nasseef)**, built in 1872 CE for Omar Nasseef Efendi, stands as the most iconic residence, having served as the royal headquarters for King Abdulaziz ibn Saud upon his entry into Jeddah in 1925.

---

# Architectural & Heritage Highlights

* **Nasseef House:** Spans 7 floors and 106 rooms, featuring wide low-rise staircases originally designed to allow horses to carry supplies up to the upper floors. Its courtyard features Jeddah's oldest planted neem tree.
* **Sharbatly House:** Located in Harat Al-Yaman, renowned for its massive four-story teakwood Roshan balconies and grand reception halls.
* **Baeshen House:** One of the oldest family mansions in Harat Al-Mazloom, historically serving as a salon for scholars and traders, now housing historical photography exhibits.
* **Matbouli House:** A restored coral block residence transformed into a private museum demonstrating 19th-century Hejazi domestic life and antique furnishings.
