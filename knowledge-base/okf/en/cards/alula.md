# Overview

**AlUla** is one of Saudi Arabia’s premier global cultural and heritage destinations. Historically positioned along the ancient incense and spice trade routes, AlUla served as a crucial crossroads of civilizations, hosting the Nabataean Kingdom as well as the Dadanite and Lihyanite dynasties. It is home to Saudi Arabia's first UNESCO World Heritage site: **Hegra**.

---

# Key Historical Attractions

* **Hegra (Al-Hijr):** Saudi Arabia’s first UNESCO World Heritage site, famous for **111 monumental tombs carved directly into sandstone outcrops** with exquisite Nabataean architectural craft.
* **AlUla Old Town:** A historic labyrinth of centuries-old mudbrick houses, an elevated citadel overlook, traditional souks, and cultural dining options.
* **Dadan Archaeological Site & Jabal Ikmah:** Capital of the ancient Dadanite and Lihyanite kingdoms, features the **Lion Tombs** and Jabal Ikmah, an **open-air library of rock inscriptions**.
* **Elephant Rock (Jabal AlFil):** A natural 52-meter-high geological wonder sculpted by wind erosion into the shape of a massive elephant standing amidst golden desert sands.
* **Maraya Hall:** A modern architectural masterpiece clad in 9,740 square meters of mirror panels, holding the Guinness World Record for the world's largest mirrored building, hosting international concerts and summits.

# Festivals & Events (AlUla Moments)

**AlUla Moments** presents a year-round calendar of international events, including:
* **Winter at Tantora:** The flagship cultural festival celebrating music, arts, heritage, and gastronomy.
* **AlUla Pulse:** Contemporary music and artistic experiences set against dramatic natural backdrops.
* **Azimuth Festival:** An immersive music and arts gathering bringing together regional and international talent at sunrise and sunset.

# Accommodation & Hospitality

AlUla offers eco-luxurious resorts and boutique lodges embedded directly into sandstone canyons and palm oases:
* **Our Habitas AlUla:** An eco-friendly luxury resort centered on wellness, sustainability, and silence.
* **Banyan Tree AlUla:** Luxury villas with private plunge pools offering dramatic canyon vistas.
* **Shaden Resort:** Premium desert resort located near Elephant Rock.
* **Palm Grove Eco-Lodges & Heritage Camps:** Authentic stay choices tucked within date palm groves and canyon retreats.

---

# Transportation & Access

* **By Air:** Direct flights to **AlUla International Airport (ULH)** from major hubs including Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dubai.
* **By Road:** 
  * A 3-hour scenic drive from **Madinah**.
  * A 6-hour drive from **Tabuk** through desert landforms.
* **Local Mobility:** Taxis, ride-hailing services, guided walking tours, and e-bike desert excursions with local storytelling guides.

---

# Practical Travel Tips

* **Optimal Timing:** Plan site visits for **early morning or golden hour before sunset** to avoid peak heat and capture light for photography.
* **Attire & Gear:** Wear comfortable walking shoes suitable for sand and stone, sunglasses, sun protection, and carry hydration.
* **Advance Bookings:** Guided tours for Hegra, Old Town, and Maraya performance tickets require **advance reservation** via official platforms.
* **Heritage Preservation:** Follow local guidelines to preserve fragile ancient rock inscriptions and archaeological monuments.
