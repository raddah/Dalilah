# Summary

**Dadan** and **Jabal Ikmah** reflect the deep pre-Nabataean heritage of AlUla. Dadan served as the capital of the Dadanite and subsequent Lihyanite kingdoms during the 1st millennium BCE, ranking among the most developed ancient trade centers in the Arabian Peninsula.

---

# Archaeological Highlights

* **The Lion Tombs of Dadan:** Monumental square tombs carved high into red sandstone cliff faces, guarded by relief sculptures of lions representing royal power and prestige.
* **Jabal Ikmah (Open-Air Library):** A secluded desert canyon containing **hundreds of carved Dadanite, Lihyanite, Minaean, and Thamudic inscriptions**, recording tax laws, religious offerings, rituals, and trade routes.
* **UNESCO Memory of the World:** Jabal Ikmah is listed on the **UNESCO Memory of the World Register** for its extraordinary linguistic and epigraphic significance in pre-Islamic Arabia.

# Visiting Logistics

* **Guided Excursions:** Visited via shuttle buses starting from the Dadan Visitor Center, accompanied by local expert storytellers ("Rawi") translating scripts and explaining ancient Kingdom history.
